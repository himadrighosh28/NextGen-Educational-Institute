OUR CURRICULUM

NextGen Educational Institute is a premier independent co-educational institution committed to providing quality education from Pre-Primary (Play Group, Nursery, LKG and UKG) to Class XII. Our academic programme is designed in accordance with the curriculum and standards of the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), preparing students for the ICSE and ISC examinations. Along with a strong academic foundation, we offer a wide range of co-curricular and extracurricular activities that support the holistic development of every learner. Through dedicated faculty, modern teaching methodologies, and a student-centric approach, we strive to nurture knowledge, confidence, creativity, and character. At NextGen Educational Institute, we blend strong values with innovation to create an environment where young minds flourish and aspirations take flight.
