PRIVACY POLICY

At NextGen Educational Institute, we respect the privacy of our students, parents, applicants, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information provided through our website.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect personal information voluntarily submitted through our website, including names, guardian details, addresses, dates of birth, email addresses, phone numbers, and other information provided through admission applications or contact queries.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

The information provided through our website is used only for legitimate institutional purposes, such as responding to enquiries, processing admission applications, communicating relevant information, and improving our services.

PROTECTION OF INFORMATION

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information submitted through our website and restrict access to authorised personnel only.

SHARING OF INFORMATION

NextGen Educational Institute does not sell, rent, or trade personal information submitted through this website. Information may only be shared when required for legitimate institutional purposes or when required by applicable law.

UPDATES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

CONTACT US

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact NextGen Educational Institute using the contact details provided on our Contact Us page.
